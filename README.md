# BLUEPRINT CLEANER

* This mod adds a little QoL to your **Crafting/Cooking Panels**.
* It lets you **hide/display the Blueprint/recipe you want** whenever you want to keep your panels as clean as possible.

## EXTENDED VIEW (VANILLA) & COMPACT VIEW (MOD)

* A key *('H' by default)* lets you toggle between the two views (Vanilla VS Mod).

## BLUEPRINTS ARE STACKED BY RESULT
* Blueprints/recipes are stacked according to **Crafting/Cooking Result**, even when they are crafted at **different crafting locations** (workbench, forge, ammo workbench, fire, anywhere...).
* They can be identified at the end of their name as follows: *[1/3]* (which means the 1st blueprint among 3... no kidding...)
* When a stack mixes crafting locations, the location of the displayed blueprint is added: *[2/4 · Forge]*
* You can switch between them with *('Left arrow' and 'Right arrow' by default)*.
* Arrows only cycle through the blueprints the **current view** lists: in a crafting location view (e.g. at the workbench), only that location's blueprints are counted and cycled. The *All* view stacks them all.
* The list stays where it is when you switch blueprints (no more jumping to the bottom of the window).

## HIDE/SHOW BLUEPRINTS
* You can hide blueprints/recipes in both views *('Left ALT' + 'LMB' by default)*
* Hidden blueprints will appear with a red background in the extended view.
* You can then display it again by clicking the same way on it. *('Left ALT' + 'LMB' by default)*

## DEBUG LOGGING
* A **Debug logging** option in the mod settings (off by default) writes stacking, cycling and scroll details to the MelonLoader log.
* Leave it off unless you are troubleshooting.
